# [Munus v1.2 - All major bugs resolved](https://github.com/jabbott-iii/Munus/releases/tag/1.2)

Thank you for your patience!

## What’s in v1.2

- Fixed submit causing TUI closure.
- Fixed crash caused by long title names.
- Connected form and list TUIs for a seamless flow.
- Fixed delete menu appearing off-screen.
- Changed selector color for better visibility.
- Improved readability.
- Fixed description not expanding correctly.

## Roadmap

v1.2 wraps up all of the major bug fixing required post v1.0 release. We can now focus on our feature additions over resolving bugs.
